UNDERSTANDING THE COST

What does rehab in Sri Lanka cost?

Transparent pricing, honest comparisons, and a clear explanation of what you get for your money.

The all-inclusive price

The cost of residential treatment at a quality clinic in Sri Lanka starts from $9,900 USD per month. That number can feel surprising to people coming from the UK, Australia or the UAE, where comparable private residential rehab typically costs two to four times as much. The natural question is: what is the catch? The honest answer is that there is not one. The lower price reflects Sri Lanka's cost of living, not a lower standard of care.

It is worth being specific about what that price actually covers, because the way rehab is priced matters as much as the headline figure. Many clinics in Western markets quote a base rate and then bill separately for individual therapy sessions, medical assessments, medications, and additional activities. By the time you add those line items, the final invoice can be substantially higher than the advertised figure. In Sri Lanka, the programmes we work with operate on a genuine all-inclusive basis.

What is included in the monthly fee

When we say all-inclusive, we mean it. The monthly programme fee covers your private en-suite accommodation for the full duration of your stay. All meals, prepared daily by kitchen staff who will accommodate dietary requirements and preferences, are included. Individual therapy sessions with a qualified counsellor or psychotherapist are scheduled throughout your programme. Group therapy, which is a central part of any evidence-based treatment approach, is included as standard. Holistic and complementary activities, including yoga, meditation, mindfulness sessions, and physical exercise, form part of the daily schedule and are not charged separately.

The fee also covers your initial medical assessment on arrival, which is essential for safe detoxification planning. Airport transfer to and from the clinic is arranged as part of your admission. And importantly, your aftercare plan, which is prepared in the final week of treatment, is part of the programme rather than an add-on service.

What is not included

Transparency works in both directions, so it is worth being equally clear about what the monthly fee does not cover. Your flights are your own responsibility, as is comprehensive travel insurance, which we strongly recommend for any stay of 28 days or longer. Personal spending money for items such as toiletries beyond the basics, additional excursions, or personal clothing is not included. Certain prescription medications may carry an additional cost depending on your individual needs, and this will be discussed clearly at the assessment stage before you commit to a programme.

How Sri Lanka compares

To put the pricing in context: a private residential rehab programme in the UK at a reputable clinic typically costs between £10,000 and £18,000 per month. The lower end of that range tends to reflect a more limited environment, shared rooms, or fewer individual therapy hours. The upper end represents some of the better-known clinics in the country. In Australia, the equivalent range is roughly AUD 15,000 to AUD 25,000 per month. In the UAE, private residential treatment commonly runs from AED 30,000 to AED 60,000 per month.

Against those figures, Sri Lanka at from $9,900 USD per month offers a genuinely compelling proposition, particularly when you consider that the Sri Lanka price includes everything, whereas many Western clinic prices are the starting point for a more itemised billing process.

Why Sri Lanka costs less, not why it is cheaper

The distinction between "costing less" and "being cheaper" is important. Sri Lanka has a significantly lower cost of living than the UK, Australia or the UAE. Qualified clinical staff, high-quality food, and well-maintained facilities cost considerably less to provide there than they would in London or Sydney. The clinical expertise has not been discounted. Many of the therapists and counsellors working in Sri Lanka's private rehab sector have trained internationally and have years of experience treating international clients. What has changed is the overhead, not the quality.

Think of it in the same way you might think about other healthcare decisions made abroad. Medical tourism to countries with lower cost bases is well established precisely because the quality of care does not automatically correlate with the local price level.

Budgeting for a full programme

If you are working out the total cost of attending a programme, a practical budget for a 28-day stay might look like this: programme fee from $9,900 USD, return flights from the UK approximately £600 to £900, travel insurance for a 28-day trip roughly £150 to £300, and personal spending money of perhaps £200 to £400 depending on your habits. The total out-of-pocket cost for a 28-day programme from the UK is therefore broadly in the range of $11,500 to $12,500 USD equivalent, all-in. That compares very favourably with the UK domestic equivalent of £10,000 to £18,000 for the programme alone, before any travel costs.

For a 60-day or 90-day programme, the per-day cost remains consistent, and longer stays are generally viewed as offering better clinical outcomes. You can read more about how to choose the right programme length at our how long is rehab page.

Payment options

Payment is typically made in full prior to admission, or in some cases as a deposit on booking with the balance settled before arrival. We can discuss instalment arrangements in certain circumstances, and we are happy to have a candid conversation about what is financially workable for your situation. The priority is getting the right person into the right programme, and we will work with you to make that as straightforward as possible.

KEY POINTS

What you need to know about cost

What is included

The monthly fee covers your private room, all meals, individual and group therapy, holistic activities, airport transfer, medical assessment, and a full aftercare plan. There are no hidden extras on the core programme.

Cost vs UK private rehab

UK residential rehab typically costs £10,000 to £18,000 per month, often with additional charges for therapy sessions. Sri Lanka offers comparable or superior care from $9,900 USD per month, all-inclusive.

How to budget for treatment

Add flights (roughly £600 to £900 from the UK), travel insurance, and personal spending to your programme fee. The total all-in cost for a 28-day stay from the UK is typically well below the cost of a comparable UK programme alone.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions about cost

Does health insurance cover rehab in Sri Lanka?
Some international health insurance policies do cover residential addiction treatment abroad, but coverage varies significantly between providers and policies. We recommend contacting your insurer directly before making any assumptions. We can provide the documentation and invoices required to support a claim. Even where insurance does not cover the full cost, it may cover a portion, and we can help you understand what to ask for.
Can I pay in instalments?
The standard arrangement is payment in full prior to admission, with a deposit on booking and the balance settled before travel. In certain circumstances we can discuss a staged payment arrangement. Please raise this during your initial consultation and we will do what we can to find a workable solution.
What is not included in the programme fee?
Flights, travel insurance, and personal spending money are not included. Some prescription medications may carry an additional charge depending on individual clinical needs, and this will be discussed transparently before you commit. The vast majority of clients find that there are no unexpected costs during their stay.
Does a lower price mean lower quality?
Not in this context. The lower price reflects Sri Lanka's cost base, not a reduction in clinical quality. Many of the therapists working in Sri Lanka's private residential sector have trained internationally. The facilities are genuinely high quality. You are not sacrificing care by choosing Sri Lanka over a more expensive Western market. You are making a financially intelligent decision.
Is a longer programme significantly more expensive?
The monthly rate is consistent, so a 60-day programme costs roughly twice the 28-day fee, and a 90-day programme roughly three times. Research consistently shows that longer programmes produce better long-term outcomes, so the additional investment in a longer stay tends to be worthwhile. We can talk through the right length for your situation.
