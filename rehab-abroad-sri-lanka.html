Rehab abroad

Why go abroad for rehab?

Distance is not a disadvantage. For most people seeking residential treatment, getting far from home is one of the most clinically important decisions they can make.

The case for going abroad

Why leaving home is part of the treatment

There is a common assumption that treatment closer to home is better. It feels more manageable, more familiar, more connected to your support network. In practice, for most people with a serious dependency, that assumption works against recovery.

The environment where addiction develops is full of triggers: specific streets, social settings, people, routines, and emotional contexts that are all associated with use. Trying to change a deeply ingrained pattern while remaining inside the environment that shaped it is like trying to stop drinking while working in a pub. It is possible, but it is much harder than it needs to be.

Going abroad removes those triggers entirely. The brain arrives in a new country, in a new climate, in unfamiliar surroundings, and the automatic associations that normally drive use are not activated in the same way. This is not just anecdotal. It is one of the reasons residential treatment outcomes are substantially better than outpatient treatment, and why treatment abroad has such a strong evidence base among addiction specialists.

Why Sri Lanka specifically

Sri Lanka has a combination of qualities that are genuinely difficult to find together elsewhere. The climate is warm and tropical year-round, which has real therapeutic value for people arriving depleted and exhausted from prolonged substance use. The pace of life is slower and more human-scale than most cities in the UK, Australia, or the UAE. The natural environment, from tea-covered hills to Indian Ocean coastline, provides a backdrop that supports rest and reflection.

Clinically, the programmes operating in Sri Lanka are led by internationally trained therapists and counsellors, many of whom have worked in the UK, Australia, the United States, and South-East Asia. The treatment frameworks are evidence-based: CBT, motivational interviewing, 12-step facilitation, trauma-informed care. These are not wellness retreats with therapy as an afterthought. They are residential rehabilitation programmes.

And the cost makes this accessible in a way that comparable treatment in Europe, Australia, or the Gulf simply is not. Programmes start from $9,900 USD per month, all-inclusive. For most clients, this represents a significant saving against a local private clinic, even after accounting for flights.

Addressing practical concerns

Most people have practical questions before committing to treatment abroad. How will I get there? What if something goes wrong medically? Is Sri Lanka safe? These are all reasonable questions and they all have reassuring answers. For the safety question in particular, see our dedicated page: Is Sri Lanka safe for rehab?

Flights from London are direct with SriLankan Airlines, taking approximately 10.5 hours. From other UK cities, Australia, and the UAE, excellent connections are available via Dubai, Doha, or Singapore. All arrivals are met at the airport and transferred directly to the facility. Clients do not need to navigate Sri Lanka independently.

For city-specific flight and logistics information, see our pages for clients travelling from London, Dubai, or Sydney.

Discretion as a reason in itself

For many clients, particularly those in professional roles or close-knit communities, the risk of being seen entering a local clinic is a genuine barrier to seeking help. Treatment in Sri Lanka removes that risk entirely. There is no possibility of a colleague walking past, a neighbour noticing, or a professional contact finding out. The discretion is absolute, and for many people it is the single factor that makes treatment possible when it would otherwise not be.

The main reasons

Three reasons clients choose rehab abroad

The clinical case for distance

Removing yourself from the triggers, environments, and social networks associated with use is one of the most effective things you can do for early recovery. Treatment abroad does this completely. Local treatment, however good, leaves you in proximity to the patterns you are trying to break.

Why Sri Lanka specifically

Internationally trained clinical teams, evidence-based programmes, a genuinely therapeutic tropical environment, and all-inclusive pricing from $9,900 USD per month. Sri Lanka offers a combination of quality, setting, and value that is difficult to match anywhere else in the world.

Practical logistics

Direct flights from London, connections from all major cities, English-speaking staff throughout, airport transfers arranged by the clinic, and immediate admission available. Going abroad for treatment is more straightforward than most people expect.

Common questions

Questions about rehab abroad

Will I be able to stay in contact with family while I am in Sri Lanka?
Yes. Most programmes allow regular phone or video contact with family after an initial settling-in period. The specific communication policy is discussed during the pre-admission assessment. Family therapy sessions via video call are also available in most programmes. Being in another country does not mean being cut off from the people who matter to you.
What if I need to come home early for an emergency?
Genuine family emergencies can always be accommodated. The clinical team will work with you and your family to manage any situation that arises. Clients are not prisoners, and no responsible programme would prevent someone from returning home in a real emergency. That said, premature discharge for non-emergency reasons is discouraged, as it significantly reduces the likelihood of sustained recovery.
Is going abroad for rehab a sign that things are very serious?
Not necessarily. Going abroad for treatment is a pragmatic decision made for many different reasons: cost, discretion, the desire for distance from a triggering environment, or simply because the best available option happens to be in Sri Lanka. It does not mean the situation is more serious than someone who treats locally. Many clients who go abroad are high-functioning people who have decided to take the problem seriously before it becomes a crisis.
How does aftercare work when I return home?
A structured aftercare plan is prepared before discharge. This includes specific recommendations for local support in your home city or country, referrals to therapists where relevant, guidance on mutual aid (AA, NA, SMART Recovery), and a relapse prevention plan. The transition home is planned and supported, not left to chance. See our aftercare page for more detail.
How quickly can I be admitted?
For most clients, admission can be arranged within 5 to 10 days of initial enquiry. This depends on the assessment process, bed availability, and how quickly travel can be arranged. This speed of admission is one of the significant advantages of treatment abroad compared to NHS services, where waits of 12 months or more are common, and some private UK clinics also operate waiting lists.
